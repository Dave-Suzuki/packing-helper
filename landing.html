Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No! TrunkPack works entirely in your browser with no sign-up required. Your data is stored locally on your device and never sent to our servers.

Is TrunkPack really free?

Yes, 100% free with no hidden costs, subscriptions, or premium tiers. All features are available to everyone.

What units of measurement are supported?

TrunkPack supports inches, centimeters, and millimeters. You can easily switch between units at any time.

Can I use this on my phone?

Absolutely! TrunkPack is fully responsive and works great on phones, tablets, and desktops. Use it wherever you need it.

Does it work offline?

Yes! Once loaded, TrunkPack works offline. Your settings are saved locally so you can access them anytime.

How accurate is the packing algorithm?

TrunkPack uses a sophisticated 3D bin-packing algorithm that considers box rotation, weight distribution strategies, and space optimization. While perfect packing is mathematically complex, our algorithm provides excellent real-world results that you can manually fine-tune.
